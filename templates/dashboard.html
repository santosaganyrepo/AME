Welcome to EduMark AI
An intelligent exam marking assistant built for teachers. Save hours of manual grading with AI-powered analysis.

Quick Start Guide

1
Configure Your Settings
Visit Settings to choose your language, marking strictness, and export format.
2
Setup a Marking Session
Go to Mark Exams — enter class, term, subject, and upload the question paper and marking scheme.
3
Upload Student Scripts

Review & Export

How to Mark Exams
Step-by-step breakdown of the AI marking workflow.
1Setup Session
Click Mark Exams in the sidebar. Fill in year, term, class, stream, subject, and exam type. Upload your question paper and marking scheme. Lock the session.
2Add Students
Enter Student ID and Full Name for each student. Capture their answer sheet pages using the camera or upload photos. Click Upload & Queue for Marking.
3AI Marks Instantly
The AI reads the script against your marking scheme and returns a score with diagnostic feedback in seconds. Results are saved automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions
Quick answers to the most common questions.
How accurate is the AI marking?
EduMark AI uses Gemini Flash. Accuracy depends on your marking scheme clarity. With a well-defined scheme the AI achieves high consistency. Balanced mode is recommended for most exams.
What file formats can I upload?
Supported: JPEG, PNG, and PDF. Ensure scans are clear and well-lit. You can also use the live camera to capture pages directly.
Can I edit or override the AI's scores?
Currently scores are assigned by AI. Override capability is planned in a future update. The AI diagnostic notes help you understand each score.
How do I export results?
Go to View Results, open the ⋯ menu on the session you want, choose "Generate spreadsheet", select your columns and format, then download.
What if a session is not found in the spreadsheet tool?
Ensure all fields match exactly what was entered during setup — including stream (e.g. "A" not "a"), subject name, and exam type. All fields are case-sensitive.
Does column selection actually filter the downloaded file?
Yes — the column selection filters the preview immediately. The selection is also sent to the backend when you download. To fully apply it server-side, the excel_generator.py needs a minor update (the columns parameter is already passed).

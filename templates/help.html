How ExamManager works

The AI reads each script against your marking scheme and scores it stage by stage. ExamManager — never the AI — adds the marks up, and you have the final say on every result.

Marking an exam

Set up the exam

Open Mark an exam. Choose the year, term, class, stream and subject, enter the total marks for the paper, and add the question paper and marking scheme as photos or PDFs.

Add students' scripts

Photograph each student's pages with the camera, or upload them from your device. For a whole class, upload one ZIP file with a folder per student named like 001_John Doe.

Let the queue mark them

Scripts are marked in the background. You can keep adding students while earlier ones are being marked, and close the page — marking carries on.

Review, adjust and export

In Results, check anything flagged, change a mark if you disagree, then download a spreadsheet or print student reports.

Scores are coloured by band: 70% and above, 50–69%, below 50%.

Common questions

Can I change a mark the AI gave?
Yes. In Results, open the session and choose Edit on the student's row. Your mark becomes the final mark everywhere — reports and spreadsheets — and the AI's original mark is kept alongside it. A student with a teacher's mark is never re-marked by the AI.
What files can I upload?
JPG, PNG and WEBP photos, and PDF files. For the best results, photograph pages flat, in good light, with the whole page in view. Blurry or very dark pages are rejected before upload so they can be retaken.
How long does marking take?
Usually under a minute per script. Several scripts are marked at the same time. When the AI service is busy, ExamManager waits and retries automatically — you don't need to do anything.
A student shows “Failed”. What should I do?
Read the reason next to their name. If the pages were unreadable, upload them again. If the AI service was busy or its daily limit was reached, use Retry failed on the session later.
How do I add more students to an exam I already set up?
In Results, open the session's menu and choose Add students. You don't need to upload the question paper or marking scheme again.
Where is my data stored?
On the computer running ExamManager, in the uploads folder. Your administrator can make a backup at any time with python tools/backup.py.
